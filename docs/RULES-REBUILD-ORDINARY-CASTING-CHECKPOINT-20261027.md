## Implemented mechanism

Authenticated ordinary base-class spell acquisition and current preparation/known/cantrip ownership can offer explicit casting with an eligible automatic ordinary or pact slot, or a free allocated cantrip. Current source identity, edition, enabled/disabled IDs, caster ownership, quota, live edit/GM authority, pool ownership and mirrors are checked before execution. Requests reject stale/repeated payments; locked dual-resource payments and explicit recovery remain atomic. Catalog lookup hydration is shared by cold primary and homebrew loaders; old saved raw snapshots are not repaired or rewritten.

## Explicit limits

Damage, targets, materials, attack/save DC, scaling, duration, concentration and other spell effects remain manual. Unknown/homebrew class owners, subclass casting, optional or expanded class-list access and unproved extra-feature cantrip quotas are held; exact eligible homebrew spells may use a verified native base class. Manual, opaque, unlimited and feature-owned pools are not adopted. Older saved homebrew spell snapshots without row-level lookup provenance remain held. The local action contract does not claim server-authoritative multiplayer delivery or acceptance of older clients for these new actions.
