This checkpoint targets twelve exact primary-source racial identities: eleven
fixed swimming base speeds of 30 feet and one fixed climbing base speed of
20 feet. It does not certify complete racial entries or grant new whole-entry,
one-witness coverage, report, or page-completion credit.

Both original bytes and canonical parsed JSON must match. Every admitted row
requires its reviewed original clause and a fresh complete installed-normalizer
fingerprint; inventory locator hashes are not authorization.

Merfolk subraces, VGM Triton and its alignment-only MOT copy, MTF Sea Elf,

or inferred transitive parent-entry disable policy is introduced.

Of the forty-four primary movement candidates, thirty-two remain held: nine
lack a movement clause in this input, fifteen have armor or encumbrance
conditions, one has a trait-replacement choice, and seven use nonfixed speed
relationships. In particular, the four DMG swimming examples are not approved

classified as supported.

## Runtime boundary

`Derived.sourceMovement` exposes only reviewed racial **source BASE** swimming
or climbing values. It is not persisted and does not claim a final legal
movement distance. The existing walking scalar, manual walking total, card
offsets, generic effects, native envelopes, and current resources retain their
existing behavior and ownership.

Global speed buffs, slowing, equal-walk relationships, armor restrictions,
level-gated surface movement, terrain, ancestry retention, burst abilities,
action timing, and map or token execution are not automated here. A readable
trace explains this limit. Unsupported or unauthenticated nonwalking
declarations produce a warning rather than a guessed speed.

Authorization requires the complete source header and fingerprint, coherent
unreserved identity, one quantity-one complete root racial owner, current
active ownership, canonical and selected-ID disable checks, and all reviewed
parent or copy source availability. Saved source mechanics retain the existing
character-edition policy; no additional universal edition classifier is added.
No Character persistence schema, source snapshot, grant, choice, action, or
resource state is rewritten by this pure projector.
