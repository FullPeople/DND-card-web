# Resource bookkeeping milestone — draft source checkpoint

This isolated candidate builds on the ordinary-casting, limited-source-use and
equipment-charge checkpoints. It is not merged or deployed. The existing
coverage report remains historical until this exact source has a new paired
replay; no new completion percentage is claimed here.

## Implemented boundary

- 128 source-verified, literal-attunement item routes support explicit known
  balance acquisition and fixed one-charge bookkeeping. All retain at least
  one charge; 91 investigated routes remain held.
- Current equipped and attuned marks, source eligibility, ownership, edit
  authority and resource locks are checked again at execution. Removing a
  mark or reopening a card does not refund or reinitialize a balance.
- The earlier 56 equipment-charge and 32 limited-source routes remain covered.
  Explicit authorized Undo/redo is retained, with current permission/lock
  checks and preservation of unrelated later or manual edits.
- Exact closed full-snapshot forms cover the catalog's known merged equipment
  normalization. Unknown forms and forged identity/header fields remain held.
  Legal generic weapon behavior is preserved independently of whether a
  charge action is supported.

## Known limits

This is partial bookkeeping, not 128 completed item rules. Activation effects,
spell execution, targets, damage, timing, recovery, final-charge consequences,
attunement procedure/legality and attunement-slot limits remain manual or held.
Existing manual data and unknown saved fields are not rewritten. No raw source
prose, real character cards or private test inputs are included.
