# Development scope: source-enumerated item-charge accounting

This isolated family extends physical-item charge **bookkeeping**, not item attacks, spells or effects. Its baseline is Web `2d5033c584b752e3445401351bdfb206a450da74`; the separate five-family source gate remains pinned to that unchanged baseline. This family is not merged, deployed or accepted as a complete-entry/runtime-report milestone.

## Qualified mechanism and holds

Nineteen previously held item identities were checked against five immutable original inputs and their exact copy/header/clause proofs. Sixteen owners contain nineteen qualified accounting branches. The adapter may expose only the source-enumerated branches, permitted integer fee choices and required per-use event/context confirmations. A player cannot substitute an arbitrary numeric debit or reuse a permanent activation flag as proof of a new triggering event.

All branches share the one known-balance physical-item pool. Genuine ownership, exact source and canonical/transport identity, single quantity, equipment/attunement, current edit/lock authority, mirrors and stale/duplicate protection remain necessary. Target/trigger confirmations are explicit player attestations, not automatic combat, spatial or effect validation.

The three held owners remain held: both editions of Pipes of the Sewers have unresolved playing/availability sequencing, and Stonespeaker Crystal couples expenditure to spellcasting/material substitution. The editions' distinct prerequisites are not transplanted between them. Existing unrelated generic weapon behavior is not removed merely because a charge branch is held.

Devlin's Staff, both Staff of Striking editions and Fangs of Guile retain at least one charge while their zero-balance consequences are unsupported. In particular, Fangs' source-permitted fee of three cannot currently execute with capacity three and minimum remainder one; that is an explicit partial-fee hold, not a rewritten source fee. Cabal stages use separate exact identities: changing stage suspends the old accounting rather than transferring or refilling a balance. Its recovery discrepancy is preserved, not repaired; recovery is outside this adapter.

No branch automatically applies damage, AC, immunity, temporary HP, curses, item effects, spellcasting, recharge, destruction or delayed consequences. An already-paid branch does not automatically refund because its later attack/save/effect fails; intentional authorized bookkeeping Undo/redo remains a separate correction workflow.

## Persistence boundary

Opening a control, acquiring an explicitly known balance and executing old fixed-one routes do not upgrade the charge namespace. The first successful execution of a newly supported variable-fee branch upgrades it to known version 2—even if that branch's chosen source-permitted fee is one. Its new event binds units, named branch, per-use context and source policy; preceding version-1 events remain unchanged. No migration, refill or source association happens on reopen.

Older clients that do not support version 2 must preserve it inactive/read-only. Unknown future versions remain opaque and inactive, not accepted as another known version. Current source/permission/resource locks still apply to explicit Undo/redo; unrelated later/manual edits must be retained. This is not a server-authoritative combat ledger or universal compatibility promise for unknown clients.
