## Qualification and supported operation

The bounded qualification examines three complete, hash-pinned original equipment inputs, not a full-catalog replay. Four authenticated maximum-HP clauses independently qualified 15 concrete owners: six generated axe forms, one named axe, and eight generated heavy-armor forms. Actual canonical normalization and cold-App equipment composition produced 30 owner-route proofs, 15 distinct complete normalized snapshots, 14 base-item proofs, three template proofs, one named-owner proof and zero normalization holds. A template or separately owned base item does not grant its generated effect. Unresolved or mismatched owners remain held.

The operation is `constant + current valid character level`, with a source-qualified constant of zero or ten and coefficient one. The axe HP clause requires current attunement, not an invented wield/wear condition. The heavy-armor clause requires current wearing and attunement and follows the existing armor-slot conflict predicate. Original source identity, edition, pack, transport revision, complete normalized fingerprints and base/template/named provenance remain separate. Provenance itself does not create extra source-switch or separately owned-record requirements.

## Consent, ownership and persistence

- `item-affine-hp:acknowledgements` is a separate version-one answer namespace. Existing native `source-level-hp` records and protocols are not rewritten.
- Each exact owned physical root needs explicit confirmation that its benefit is not already included in manual HP. Confirmation writes only this namespace, never an effect value, source field, HP roll, current/temporary HP or resource.
- A changed manual HP basis invalidates confirmation. A manual-final HP override holds the new contribution rather than guessing whether to add above the final value. Native and item consent records do not enter either manual-HP basis.
- Current valid root class levels total one through twenty. Level changes rederive a still-valid benefit; item selection level does not substitute for character level.
- Quantity other than one is a conservative physical-identity hold, not a publisher stacking prohibition. Multiple currently eligible same-effect owners are held visibly where stacking is unproved; inactive stored copies do not cancel the single valid owner. No quantity multiplication or speculative additive/maximum policy is introduced.
- Unattuning, removing required wear or disabling the actual source pauses the benefit without deleting consent or healing. An unchanged exact state can reuse its prior explicit consent; no new confirmation is automatically written. Withdrawal and a newly selected physical UID do not automatically reconfirm.
- Live edit/GM authority and existing card-lock policy gate mutation. Stale/repeated requests are rejected without rewriting the card. Current-last receipts authenticate the exact request fingerprint and card/owner/history. Future, malformed or foreign-card histories are preserved read-only and inactive.
